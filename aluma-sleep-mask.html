How does red-light therapy work?

Red-light therapy in the Aluma Sleep Mask emits low-wavelength red light to relax your mind and body. It reduces exposure to blue light, which can disrupt sleep, and supports your natural circadian rhythm. By minimizing blue light, it allows your body to produce melatonin more effectively, helping you fall asleep faster and improve sleep quality.
